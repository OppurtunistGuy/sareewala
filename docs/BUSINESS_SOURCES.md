## Reviews

The aggregate rating above is Google's published rating, not a calculation from selected excerpts. The listing exposed five review entries. Relevant comments are mixed:

- **K V**, 4/5: “U will find wide variety of sarees here..just bargain”
- **Sanjay Palande**, 4/5: “Very nice products”
- **Pravin Shantaram Joshi**, 2/5: “Costly and varities are less”
- **SUMIT DUBEY**, 2/5: praised the varieties but complained about pricing and customer service.

A further review described a packers-and-movers service, so it was excluded from the qualitative summary as unrelated to a saree shop. Its presence was not used to alter Google's aggregate rating.

The storefront displays only the attributed aggregate and a link to read the original reviews. It does not label reviewers as verified purchasers or present a selected positive comment as representative of all customers. Business structured data includes the address, map link, coordinates and hours, but does not claim review-rich-result eligibility.

## Other listings and access limits

Justdial requests returned an HTTP 403 CONNECT denial from the cloud egress proxy. No Justdial listing, rating, phone number, or opening-hours claim could be verified. Google Search returned a JavaScript access page; Bing results did not identify a reliably matching listing. The supplied Google Maps link was subsequently accessible and provided the facts above.

The draft network allowlist now includes justdial.com and www.justdial.com, preserving existing custom entries. Saving a draft alone does not apply it to the running machine. Justdial verification remains pending access being enabled through environment settings.
